---

## The Essential Freelancer Protections

1. **Payment-Triggered IP Assignment:** IP transfer must strictly occur upon receipt of full and final payment.
2. **Aggregate Liability Caps:** Total contractor liability must be capped at 100% of fees paid under the specific Statement of Work.
3. **Deemed Acceptance Windows:** Client has a finite inspection period (5-10 business days) to submit written objective defect notices.
4. **Pre-Existing IP & Starter Kit Retention:** Explicit exclusion of developer starter templates, libraries, and proprietary tooling from client assignment.
5. **Clear Change Order Procedures:** Subjective scope expansions require written agreement and separate milestone compensation.
